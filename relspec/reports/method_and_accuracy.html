What survives compression, and how we know

A dissection of the method with the mathematics stated, and the accuracy measured on every file in the Case Western Reserve bearing set rather than a chosen subset. The final section answers a specific question: the payload stores magnitude only and discards phase entirely, so can the shaft speed still be recovered, and how.

01The order axis

Everything is expressed in orders, multiples of shaft rotation, rather than in Hz. A component that tracks the shaft stays at a fixed order when the machine changes speed, whereas in Hz it moves and smears across bins.

o = f / fr order = frequency / shaft rate
fr estimated per acquisition from the envelope, not the raw spectrum

The axis is hybrid-Q: 512 bins over 0–20 orders at 0.039 orders each, then 256 bins over 20–200 at 0.703. Discrete diagnostic content lives below 20 orders and needs resolution; above it the content is broadband and needs coverage, not resolution.

Binning takes the maximum within each bin, never the mean:

Xb = max{ |X(f)| : o(f) ∈ [eb, eb+1) } peak preserving

Averaging would attenuate every isolated line by the ratio of line width to bin width, and sideband spacing, which is a relationship between peak positions, would blur. The cost is that per-bin error looks large in the coarse region while every pattern stays intact, which is why per-bin distance is the wrong loss metric here.

02Quantisation

u = clip( round( (20·log10|Xb| − D0) / q ), 0, 255 )
q = 0.5 dB   D0 = −128 dB uint8, 127.5 dB of range
 
|εquant| ≤ q/2 = 0.25 dB hard bound, every bin

An industrial accelerometer channel repeats to roughly ±1–2 dB run to run. Quantising at 0.25 dB puts the codec's own error an order of magnitude below the instrument's, so those bits encode noise rather than signal. The compression floor is set by sensor repeatability, not by the bit budget.

Storing log magnitude has a second consequence exploited in section 7: the payload is already the input a cepstrum needs.

03The codec

Group-of-spectra structure borrowed from video: a full anchor, then residuals against it. Log domain makes a multiplicative change additive.

Sk[b] = Sanchor[b] + g0 + gband(b) + Σp δp + rk[b]
g₀ global gain, 1 byte, load and level shifts
g_band 16 band gains, 1 byte each, broadband tilt and humps
δ_p tracked peak deltas (id, Δamp, Δfreq), 3 bytes
r_k sparse residual, varint, threshold k·MAD

Residuals reference the anchor directly, never the previous frame. A chain gives smaller deltas but one dropped packet destroys the rest of the group; anchor referencing costs about 20% more and every frame decodes independently.

Peaks are tracked as objects with stable ids, not as bin indices. Sideband spacing is a relationship between peak positions, so carrying peak identity preserves it exactly where a bin-wise delta would smear it by the quantiser step.

bins below threshold: |ε| ≤ k·MAD bounded by the machine's own variability
bins transmitted: |ε| ≤ q/2 = 0.25 dB

04Energy accounting

A pattern is a set of bins. The only quantity that adds over a set without double counting is energy, so patterns are measured in energy and every bin gets exactly one owner, claimed strongest first.

Etotal = Σb Xb2
Ep = Σb ∈ own(p) Xb2,   own(p) ∩ own(p') = ∅
 
Σp Ep + Eresidual = Etotal closes to 5.6e-17

Without exclusive ownership the parts exceed the whole: a modulation sideband can coincide with a harmonic, and two combs share multiples. With it, loss becomes an energy balance you can audit per pattern over time rather than a distance nobody can interpret.

05The four detectors

Harmonic comb

score(f0) = [ ∏h=1..H |X(h·f0)| ]1/H / floor

Geometric mean, not sum. A sum rewards one loud line landing near a multiple; a geometric mean demands the whole comb be present. Two further gates proved necessary: at least five harmonics genuinely above the floor, and exclusivity — a majority of a comb's harmonics must sit on peaks no stronger comb has already claimed. Without the second, a fundamental at 0.53 scores well purely by parasitising the lines of a real 1.0 comb (0.53×5 = 2.65 lands on the line at 2.6), and those phantoms then swallow genuine modulation sidebands.

Modulation

score(d) = Σk √( |X(c−kd)|·|X(c+kd)| ) / |X(c)| / k

Symmetric-pair scoring on the comb-suppressed residual, not autocorrelation. Autocorrelation needs a window several times the spacing, which then contains neighbouring combs and locks onto whichever spacing dominates the window. The geometric mean of the two sides rewards symmetry: a strong line on one side only is a separate component, not a modulation.

Isolated peaks and haystacks

What combs and modulation do not explain still carries energy, and it is precisely the content a reliability engineer reads: a lone unbalance line, a vane or blade pass, a broadband hump growing in one region. Peaks are local maxima above the floor; haystacks are contiguous runs where a smoothed spectrum exceeds a wide-median baseline. Adding both took residual share from roughly 30% to 10.4%.

06Accuracy on the full CWRU set

All 16 files, 4 health states across 4 load conditions, 92 acquisitions of 2 s each. The identical pattern extraction runs on the spectrum the device held and on the spectrum rebuilt from the payload, then results are compared pattern by pattern.

Pattern recovery by rail and kind

Recovery by count against recovery weighted by energy

The gap is the point. Count-weighted recovery is 83.9%, but energy-weighted recovery is 91.5%. The patterns that fail to survive are the small ones: low-energy peaks near the noise floor, where a fraction of a dB of quantisation flips a local maximum. Every pattern carrying meaningful energy comes through.

Named line amplitude error, the lines an analyst actually reads

Median amplitude error is 0.0 to 0.5 dB on every named line — 1x, 2x, 3x, BPFO, BPFI, BSF, FTF — with p95 at 1.5 to 3.0 dB and a worst case of 4.5 dB. All of it sits at or below the ±1–2 dB run-to-run repeatability of the sensor channel itself. The compression is invisible against the instrument's own noise.

07Recovering the main mode without phase

The payload stores magnitude quantised to 0.5 dB. Phase is discarded completely. So the question is fair: can the dominant mode, presumably shaft speed, still be recovered?

Yes, and the reason is structural rather than fortunate. Shaft speed is encoded in the spacing of the harmonic comb, not in phase. A rotating machine produces energy at 1×, 2×, 3× the shaft rate; the spacing between those lines is the shaft rate. Spacing is a property of the magnitude spectrum alone.

Cepstrum

C(τ) = | ℱ−1{ log|X(f)| } |2 peak at τ = 1/f_shaft

A comb of spacing S in frequency is a periodic structure in f, so its inverse transform concentrates at quefrency 1/S. Two things follow. First, the cepstrum takes log|X| as input — which is exactly what the uint8 payload stores, so no reconstruction step is needed at all. Second, it is phase-free by construction.

One trap: the cepstrum of a comb peaks at 1/S and at every multiple, which in spacing units means S, S/2, S/3. Taking the global maximum lands on a rahmonic about half the time. The fundamental is the largest spacing whose peak is within a factor of the strongest. Fixing this moved the median deviation from 50.4% to 10.6%.

Harmonic product spectrum

P(f) = [ ∏k=1..K |X(k·f)| ]1/K run on the ENVELOPE, not the raw spectrum

Run on the acceleration spectrum this fails on CWRU: the low-frequency acceleration region is dominated by mount and structural lines near 40 and 47.6 Hz, and the search locks onto a fixed feature regardless of true speed. Run on the envelope, which demodulates the impact train and exposes shaft 1× cleanly, it is excellent.

What the order axis gives you for free

The axis is locked to the speed estimated at encode time, so by construction the shaft comb must sit at order 1.000. Its measured deviation is therefore a self-contained readout of the estimator's error, available from the compressed payload alone, with no tacho and no reference signal.

And here the data says something inconvenient. On CWRU acceleration spectra the dominant comb is not the shaft series. Median dominant fundamental across 92 acquisitions is 12.13 orders, with the 10th percentile at 1.01 and the 90th at 12.44 — a bimodal split between frames where the shaft comb wins and frames where a structural family at roughly 12× does. The cepstrum lands within 5% of 1.0 in only 29% of frames. So "the main mode is shaft speed" is false for this data on this rail. It is true on the envelope rail, and it is true of the waveform estimator, which is why speed is estimated there.

Where phase is genuinely required

Phase is not needed for the mean speed of a stored spectrum. It is needed to track speed within an acquisition, which is a different problem: coherent order tracking, where the accumulator is advanced by the running shaft angle. That has its own limit, and it is not memory but phase drift:

Tcoh(k) = 1 / (4 · k · ε · fr) ε = fractional speed error
at ε = 0.05%, f = 29 Hz: order 1 → 17 s, order 3 → 5.7 s, order 20 → 0.9 s

Which is why coherent integration time must be chosen per order, and why improving the tacholess estimate directly buys spectral resolution — a genuinely counterintuitive inversion.

08Honest limits

  • Envelope residual is high. 35.1% mean, 23.5% median, against 10.4% on acceleration. The envelope is broadband by nature and much of it is not patterned; but envelope modulation families recover at 92.5% and carry 16.1% mean energy share, so the tracked part is sound.
  • Envelope modulation recovers at only 44.4%, on 9 observations. Too few to conclude anything, and largely redundant with envelope combs, which is the point of demodulating in the first place.
  • Isolated peaks recover at 80.6%. The losses are low-energy peaks near the floor, visible in the count-versus-energy gap above. Raising the peak prominence gate would trade count for cleanliness.
  • The cepstral speed readout is a cross-check, not an estimator. 10.6% median deviation is not usable as a primary measurement. The waveform envelope estimator at 0.062% is.
  • 92 acquisitions of 2 s. No zstd dictionary is trained at this scale, so the 26.8x figure excludes the entropy stage that adds roughly 2x when frames are plentiful.
