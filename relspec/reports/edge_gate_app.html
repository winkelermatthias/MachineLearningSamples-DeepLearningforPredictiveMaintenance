With idle frames allowed into the baseline, the learned spread doubles to cover both operating states. Every start-up then reads as an anomaly, and the real fault no longer stands out against a baseline wide enough to contain it.

Running acquisitions are real CWRU 12 kHz drive-end measurements. Idle acquisitions are synthesized as a sensor noise floor plus faint structural coupling, since CWRU contains no stopped-machine data. Every decision shown was produced by the compiled C firmware, not a model of it.
