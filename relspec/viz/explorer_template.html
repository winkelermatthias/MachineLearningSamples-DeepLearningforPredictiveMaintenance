0 · the idea in one paragraph

A vibration spectrum is mostly structure: harmonic combs locked to the shaft, sidebands around carriers, a handful of lone lines, broad humps, and a noise floor that barely moves between days. relspec measures that structure explicitly, normalises the frequency axis so the structure stops moving, and then transmits only what changed — entropy-coded, with a hard bound on what may be thrown away. Everything below is computed live from the sequence and frame you have selected; change them and the numbers follow.

1 · shaft speed — the axis everything hangs on

Order normalisation divides frequency by shaft speed fr, so a line at “3.58×” stays at 3.58× while the machine speeds up. That only works if fr is right. The envelope of the bandpassed signal exposes the impact comb, and a harmonic product score over candidate speeds finds its fundamental:

HPS(f) = ( ∏h=1..H E(h·f) )1/H H = 3 harmonics, E = envelope spectrum

The product is the safety: a rival line can fake one harmonic, not three in exact ratio. A second, independent comb search on the acceleration spectrum must corroborate the envelope answer before it is trusted — a confidently wrong estimator is the failure mode a confidence threshold cannot catch.

2 · the frame: two rails, hybrid-resolution, ½ dB

Each acquisition becomes two order-binned spectra: an acceleration rail (768 bins: 512 fine bins over orders 0–20, 256 coarse to 200) and an envelope rail (384 bins over 0–20). Binning takes the MAX per bin, never the mean — averaging erases sideband spacing, which is the diagnosis. Amplitudes are stored as uint8 in 0.5 dB steps:

u = round( (20·log₁₀ a − OFF) / 0.5 ), OFF = −128 dB → quantiser error ≤ 0.25 dB

3 · the codec — anchor, residual, entropy

A GOP structure: an occasional anchor carries the whole spectrum losslessly (first-difference + adaptive Rice coding); every other frame is a residual against a reference the decoder already holds. The residual decodes in four layers — global gain, per-band gains, tracked-peak deltas, then an active-bin list — and every symbol passes through a Rice coder whose parameter adapts from a running mean identically on both sides, so it is never transmitted. Peak tables cost zero bytes: the decoder re-derives them from its bit-exact reference.

Sk[b] = clamp( Sref[b] + g₀ + gband(b) + Δpeaks + r[b] ) r[b] transmitted only where |r[b]| > max( k·MAD[b], floor ) — the dead zone

Rice-code an integer:  k

Zigzag folds the sign (quotient, unary · stop bit · k remainder bits). The coder keeps a running mean A/N per context and uses k = ⌈log₂(A/N)⌉ — small k for quiet contexts, large for loud ones, no table ever sent.

4 · the distortion contract

Transmitted bins are exact to the ½ dB quantiser. Suppressed bins may drift, but only inside the dead zone — bounded by the machine's own run-to-run noise:

|decoded − raw| ≤ max( 2.5·MAD[b], 3 dB ) on suppressed bins, 0 on transmitted ones

5 · patterns — the energy ledger

Combs are scored by the geometric mean of harmonic amplitudes over the local floor — the whole family must exist, one loud line buys nothing. Each bin's energy is then assigned to at most one owner (strongest pattern first), so pattern energies plus the residual sum exactly to the spectrum's total: a closed ledger, which is what makes “loss” a measurable number instead of a vibe.

score(f₀) = ( ∏h A(h·f₀) )1/H / floor Etotal = Σpatterns Ep + Eresidual

6 · the edge gate — anomaly events

On the device, a robust baseline (median/MAD per feature, per band, per bin) turns each frame into an anomaly score; a persistence vote, a CUSUM drift detector and a spectral band mask decide whether this frame is worth a radio session. Frames judged quiet update the baseline; frames in change never do — that one rule is what keeps a developing fault from teaching the baseline to ignore it.

z = (v − med)/1.4826·MAD score = ‖min(|z|,12)‖₃ + band-mask term + CUSUM term
